# Worthless Haunted Meat

**Technology that tears down barriers to live experiences.**

We are a Texas 501(c)(3) nonprofit that builds open-source assistive technology so that disability never decides who gets the best seat in the house. We believe the front row belongs to everyone — and that the recipes for getting people there should be free, documented, and replicable by any venue, festival, or fan with off-the-shelf hardware.

🌐 [worthlesshauntedmeat.org](https://worthlesshauntedmeat.org)

## What we build

Everything we publish here is:

- **Open source** — GPL-3.0, so improvements stay open
- **Cost-effective** — consumer hardware, free software, published parts lists
- **Replicable** — step-by-step guides, not just code; if you can't rebuild it from our repos, we haven't finished it
